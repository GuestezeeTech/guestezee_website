FAQ

What is GuestEzee?

GuestEzee is the world's first real-time Guest Management System (GMS) that bridges hotel operations with guest satisfaction, simplifying workflows, enhancing guest experiences, and boosting revenue through a single ecosystem.

How is GuestEzee different from a PMS?

While a PMS handles bookings, GuestEzee enhances the guest journey by enabling mobile check-in, keyless access, real-time service, escalations, and AI-driven feedback. It complements your PMS, not replaces it.

Can GuestEzee integrate with our PMS and OTA systems?

Yes, GuestEzee integrates seamlessly with major PMS platforms, OTA channels, payment gateways, and door lock systems, eliminating the need to change your core systems.

How simple is the onboarding process?

Onboarding is straightforward. Register on the website, complete the form, make payment, and upon approval, your Hotel Admin Dashboard will appear. You can onboard staff, configure services, and go live instantly with no setup hassles.

Is there a separate app for hotel staff?

Yes, there's a dedicated Staff App for Android and iOS. Admins add staff via the dashboard, and staff can log in to respond to guest requests in real-time, streamlining communication across departments.

How do guests access the GuestEzee experience?

Guests can download the Guest App from the App Store or Google Play to check in, access rooms, request services, order food, book the spa, and check out — all from their mobile phone.

How does GuestEzee help with guest reviews?

GuestEzee uses AI to track guest experiences and generate feedback prompts. The feedback is sent to guests for approval before being pushed to OTAs, ensuring authentic reviews.

What kind of analytics and insights do we get?

You'll get a comprehensive Hotel Admin Dashboard with guest engagement scores, staff performance tracking, revenue insights, escalation alerts, and real-time service requests, enabling smarter, faster decisions.

Is GuestEzee suitable for all hotel types?

Yes, GuestEzee is a global solution that can be adopted by any hotel, group, or brand, no matter the size — from small boutique hotels to large chains.

Does GuestEzee support sustainability?

Yes, GuestEzee promotes paperless operations and replaces plastic key cards with secure mobile-based digital keys, contributing to greener hotel practices.

Additional Information

GuestEzee operates on an annual subscription basis, providing the complete tech stack — Hotel Admin, Staff App, and Guest App — so hotels get everything needed for seamless operations and guest experience management.
